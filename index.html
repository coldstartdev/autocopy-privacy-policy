AutoCopy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

AutoCopy does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data lives exclusively in your own browser's Chrome sync storage.

What AutoCopy Does

AutoCopy is a Chrome extension for Gmail that automatically adds BCC and CC recipients to outgoing emails based on rules you define. Rules are evaluated entirely on your device — no email content, addresses, or metadata is ever sent anywhere outside your browser.

Data We Collect

AutoCopy collects no personal data. The only information the extension stores is the rules you create yourself, which may include:

  • Rule names you assign
  • Email addresses you enter as BCC/CC targets
  • Matching conditions (recipient addresses or domains, subject keywords, schedule)
  • Your enabled/disabled preference for the extension

This data is stored exclusively using the browser's built-in chrome.storage.sync API, which means it is synced across your own devices through your personal Google Chrome account — the same way your bookmarks and settings sync. It is never transmitted to any server operated by AutoCopy or any third party.

Permissions Explained

  • storage — Required to save and sync your rules across Chrome devices using the browser's own sync infrastructure.
  • Access to mail.google.com — Required to detect when a Gmail compose window opens and to inject BCC/CC recipient chips into it. AutoCopy reads only the To/Subject fields needed to evaluate your rules, and writes only to the BCC/CC fields. No email content is read, stored, or transmitted.

Third-Party Services

AutoCopy does not use any analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or other third-party services. There are no external network requests of any kind.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise share any data. Because no data is collected by us, there is nothing to share.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your rules are stored in chrome.storage.sync and persist until you delete them through the extension's Manage Rules page, or until you uninstall the extension. Uninstalling AutoCopy removes all locally stored data. You can also delete individual rules or use the "Clear All Rules" option at any time.

Children's Privacy

AutoCopy is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is ever updated, the revised date at the top of this page will reflect the change. Significant changes will also be noted in the extension's update release notes.
